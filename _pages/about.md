I am a Phd student at IMFT in Toulouse in the MIR group and affiliated to the "Université de Toulouse". 
My research focuses on hydrogen combustion in aircraft injectors, specifically investigating dynamics such as:
- Lift-off and anchoring
- Low and high frequency thermoacoustic instabilities
- Combustion noise
